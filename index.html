How to play

Represent the inequality accurately. Check the endpoints, the circles and the direction of the shading. Each correct answer completes another part of the picture.

Open circle

The endpoint is excluded: < or >.

Closed circle

The endpoint is included: ≤ or ≥.

↔
Between two endpoints

Place both circles and shade the segment between them.

◧
Mirror Mode

Represent one-sided inequalities using the correct circle and arrow. Complete all the questions to reveal the picture.

Click or tap a circle to switch between open and closed. Drag it to move its endpoint, tap a number-line tick, or use the +/− buttons. After a correct check, the next question appears automatically. When dividing an inequality by a negative number, reverse the signs.
