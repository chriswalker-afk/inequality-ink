How does this challenge work?

Plot: Re(z) goes across, Im(z) goes up. Transform: conjugation reflects; multiplying by i rotates through 90° anticlockwise. Modulus: |z − c| is distance from c. Argument: arg(z − c) is the angle measured from c. A strict inequality has a dashed boundary.
